Model assumptions

Read these before interpreting results.

  • Instantaneous, fixed irradiance across the stated PV area (W/m² × m² = W).
  • Constant PV and inverter efficiencies at the chosen settings.
  • No battery, grid exchange, clipping, cable losses, temperature corrections, or irradiance transients.
  • AC power represents ideal instantaneous real power available to a matching load; no energy-yield forecast is made.
  • Energy (Wh or kWh) would require integrating power over time; this page calculates power only (W).
